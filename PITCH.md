# BAHALA NA? — One-Page Pitch

**Format:** mobile narrative roguelite · 10–12 min per run · Android first, iOS second
**Team:** small (design + backend + frontend + writer) · **Target:** playable demo + pitch by 2026-12-09

## Logline
Grow up in a fictional Philippine barangay one card at a time. Every easy shortcut quietly builds a hidden debt; every hard moment is a d20 roll. Alone, the system is unbeatable — together, it isn't. Every run permanently changes a **shared barangay** that the next player inherits.

## The hook
- **Temptation that works.** "Pang-merienda lang." "Ako na bahala, Tita." Shortcuts feel reasonable, help immediately, and are never labeled as anything ugly — until the *Singil* card calls the debt in at 32.
- **A rigged system you can feel.** Solo rolls against systemic DCs are ~25%. With 2–3 assists, ~60–70%. The math *is* the message: *bahala na* looks different when people roll together.
- **Asynchronous multiplayer without servers in the way.** Your run leaves echoes, trust, and reform progress in a barangay every later player inherits. Eras shift from **Mabulok** to **Bagong Umaga** as the community wakes up.
- **A real-world ending.** The final screen shows your hidden debt ledger, your barangay impact ("37% of players spoke up — the tipping point is 40%"), and one concrete, location-specific action: *attend your next barangay assembly, ask to see the posted budget, bring one neighbor.*

## Content
40 hand-written Taglish cards across 4 chapters (childhood → crossroads → duty → the flood), 5 careers (including the Ordinary Citizen, whose *number* matters most), 10 humanizing endings, 12 pre-written echoes. No real politicians, parties, or agencies. No preaching — consequences teach.

## Why now / why us
- Short, replayable, mobile-first: a complete life in a commute.
- Firebase + Addressables = live-ops-friendly; balance and content tunable without store review.
- Built for AI-assisted collaboration: content as JSON, frozen API contract, decision log.

## The ask
Playtest (5–10 people), then build the Unity client against the frozen contract in `API_CONTRACT.md`. Demo first, polish after the paper playtest validates temptation, Singil fairness, and the climax.
